The Australian Lipid Society's flagship activity is the **Australian Lipid Meeting (ALM)**, held biennially. ### ICBL 2027: Harbouring Lipid Innovation

![ICBL 2027 banner](/images/ICBL_poster.pdfs)

**19-22 July 2027 | The University of Sydney, Camperdown, New South Wales**

The 67th International Conference on the Bioscience of Lipids (ICBL) will be held in Sydney, and it is the first time this long-running meeting has been hosted in Australia. The conference is organised with the support of the Australian Lipid Society.

[Visit the conference website →](https://icbl2027.org/)

---

### About ICBL

The International Conference on the Bioscience of Lipids (ICBL) is an international, non-profit forum for scientists working in lipid research. Founded in 1953 in Brussels, it brings together established and early-career researchers to present and discuss the latest developments in the chemical, biological, nutritional and clinical aspects of lipids, in a friendly environment that encourages new collaborations. The 66th ICBL was held in Bordeaux, France, in September 2026, jointly with the French Lipid Research Society (GERLI).

### Venue

The conference will take place at the University of Sydney's central Camperdown campus, close to the city, harbour and airport. Sydney is well served by public transport, and the venue is about a 10 minute bus ride from the city.
