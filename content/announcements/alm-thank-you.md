The Australian Lipid Meeting 7, held jointly with the 5th International Lipidomics Society Conference, took place in Perth on 18-21 October 2026.

The Society sincerely thanks the invited speakers, delegates, sponsors, the organising committee and the International Lipidomics Society for making the first joint meeting of the two societies possible.

We look forward to seeing the community again at [ICBL 2027 in Sydney](/events/).
