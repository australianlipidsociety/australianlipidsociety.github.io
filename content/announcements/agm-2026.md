The Society held its Annual General Meeting on Monday 19 October 2026 at the Novotel Perth Langley, during the Australian Lipid Meeting 7 and 5th iLS Conference. The key outcomes are below.

### The Society

- The Society was incorporated in Western Australia on 12 February 2026 under the *Associations Incorporation Act 2015*.
- Membership had reached **84 active members**. Membership remains free.
- The President, Secretary and Treasurer reports were received, and the Treasurer is completing the financial handover to the incoming committee.

### Constitution amended

Members unanimously approved a special resolution to the constitution. Nominations for all committee positions can now be made either in writing before the AGM or from the floor of the AGM, provided the nomination is seconded by a member present.

### Committee elected

**Office holders**

- **Dr Luke Whiley**, President
- **Prof Anthony Don**, Vice-President
- **Dr Dana Hicks**, Secretary
- **A/Prof Jacob Qi**, Treasurer

**General committee members**

- **Dr Monique Ryan**
- **Dr Kevin Huynh**

Members acknowledged the work of the inaugural committee in establishing the Society and delivering ALM7.

### Looking ahead

- The Society will provide financial support for the Sydney meeting in July 2027, organised by Anthony Don.
- Australian Lipid Meetings are generally held every two years, in alternate years to the Singapore Lipid Symposium (SLING). Members are encouraged to consider hosting a future meeting after Sydney and to tell the President or Vice-President about potential host cities.
- The constitution allows the Society to support activities beyond the ALM, such as community-building and scientific initiatives. These would be funded from surplus conference revenue, as membership will remain free.

### Help us document the history of the ALM

The Society plans to publish a history of the Australian Lipid Meetings, including the six meetings held before the Society was formed. If you have details of earlier meetings, including those held in Adelaide and Brisbane, please email them to <australianlipidsociety@gmail.com>.
