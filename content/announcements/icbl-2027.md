---

The Society is delighted to share that the **67th International Conference on the Bioscience of Lipids (ICBL 2027)**, "Harbouring Lipid Innovation", will be held at the University of Sydney from **19-22 July 2027**.

ICBL was founded in 1953 and is one of the world's longest-running forums for lipid research. This will be the first time the meeting has been hosted in Australia, and the Society is supporting the conference.

For abstract and registration updates, visit the [conference website](https://icbl2027.org/) or see our [Events page](/events/).
